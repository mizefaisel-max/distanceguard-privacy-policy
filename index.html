1. Introduction

DistanceGuard ("the App") is a screen-health and eye-protection app that helps users maintain a safe viewing distance from their screens and manage screen time. It's built for anyone, and includes optional supervision features for a parent or guardian managing a shared or child's device. This policy describes what the App accesses on your device, how it's used, and what happens to it. The App has no user accounts, no backend server, and no third-party analytics — so there is nothing for anyone to collect in the first place.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up, or login of any kind
  • No server that receives data from the App — we do not operate one
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools
  • We never sell, rent, or share information with anyone, because none leaves your device

3. Camera & proximity detection

DistanceGuard uses your device's camera to estimate how close a face is to the screen, using on-device face detection that runs locally on the device. Camera frames are processed in real time and discarded immediately — they are never saved as a photo or video, never uploaded, and never viewed by anyone, including us.

4. Screen-time & violation data

The App keeps a local log of screen-time usage and "too close" events so users can review them in the History screen. For the optional per-app time-limit feature, the App uses Android's Usage Access permission to check which app is currently in the foreground, so it can enforce the daily limits set for specific apps (such as Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok). This check happens entirely on-device — the App only reads the name of the currently active app to compare it against your configured limits, and never records or transmits your general app-usage history. All of this data stays in the App's local storage on the device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed if the App is uninstalled.

5. Security & Settings PIN

The App asks the user or a parent to set a 4-digit PIN. In Child and Teen mode, the PIN protects app settings and approves extra time when a time limit is reached. The PIN is stored locally on the device and is never transmitted anywhere.

6. Quick Launch & in-app browser

The App's Quick Launch grid gives shortcuts to sites such as YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram, opening either the installed app or DistanceGuard's own in-app browser. Once you leave DistanceGuard for one of these destinations, that service's own privacy policy applies to what happens there. DistanceGuard does not transmit that browsing activity to us or to any third party.

7. App permissions

  • Camera — for proximity/distance detection
  • Display over other apps — to show the too-close warning and "Time's Up" screen even while another app is open
  • Notifications — to show the App's status and alerts
  • Usage Access — to detect which app is currently open, so the optional per-app time-limit feature can enforce the limits you set for specific apps
  • Internet — to load websites in the in-app browser and Quick Launch. DistanceGuard itself does not send your data anywhere.
  • Foreground service (camera / special use) — to keep eye-distance protection and the "Time's Up" screen running reliably while you use other apps. A persistent notification is shown while it runs.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — to ask Android not to stop that protection service in the background.
  • Receive boot completed — to restart protection after the device restarts.

Each permission is used only for the purpose listed above.

8. Children's privacy

DistanceGuard is intended to be installed and configured by an adult. When used to help supervise a shared or child's device, all monitoring stays local — DistanceGuard does not knowingly send any personal information about a child, or any user, to us or to any third party.

9. Data security

Because DistanceGuard doesn't transmit data anywhere, its protection depends on the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device's lock screen and the App's PIN secure, since anyone with access to an unlocked device could see the same local data you can.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version. We encourage checking back periodically.
